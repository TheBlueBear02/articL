# Short-video structure research (Reels / TikTok), Nov 2026

Researched 2026-11-19 to decide how ArticL reels should be structured. **Source quality:** only two sources here are primary: TikTok's own ads help page, and Instagram head Adam Mosseri's statements, which come to us through press coverage. Everything else is creator-tool or agency blogs. Their numbers disagree and are mostly unverified, so treat them as directional. Anything marked *(unverified)* could not be traced to a platform source.

## 1. What the platforms actually reward

- **Instagram:** Mosseri names three signals: **watch time**, **likes per reach** and **sends per reach** (shares by DM). He says sends matter slightly more for reaching non-followers. He has given no ranking order between them. In April 2026 Insights added share rate and skip rate.
- **Retention beats length:** a short reel that people finish beats a long one they abandon. Replays count as watch time, which is why loops matter.
- **Saves** are repeatedly cited as strong for educational content *(platform weight unverified)*.
- **Originality:** Instagram demotes unoriginal or reposted content from recommendations to non-followers. Several blogs say templated "faceless stock-footage" reels are the most exposed. Instagram says it does not penalise AI use itself, but AI content may be labelled ("AI info"). *Reports conflict; no confirmed reach penalty for AI.*
- **TikTok as search:** TikTok indexes spoken words and on-screen text, so key terms should be both said and shown.

## 2. The structure that keeps coming up

| Time | Element | What it does |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0–1 s | **Visual + text hook** | Bold on-screen text and a striking first frame, with no intro, logo or fade from black. TikTok's own guidance is to state the proposition in the first 3 s. Blogs push this to the first 1 s. |
| 1–3 s | **Spoken hook / promise** | A counter-intuitive claim, a surprising number, a question, or "a study of X people found…". |
| ~3 s | **Open loop** | Tease the payoff ("the third finding is the one nobody expected"), so leaving now feels like missing something. |
| body | **Beats + pattern interrupts** | A change every ~2–5 s: cut, zoom, on-screen number, sound hit. Each change should add information, not decoration. |
| ~15 s and ~30 s | **Re-hooks** | Mini cliffhangers to fight the mid-video drop ("but that's not the strange part"). |
| last 3–5 s | **Payoff + engagement prompt** | Deliver the promised payoff, then give a reason to comment, save or send ("send this to someone who…", "what do you think caused it?"). |
| end → start | **Seamless loop** | The last line and frame flow into the first, so the replay starts before the viewer notices. |

### The loop technique (the "circle" effect)

- **Script loop:** write the closing sentence so it grammatically continues into the hook. For example, closing: *"…and that's exactly why"* → hook: *"people who lose money in the market change how they vote."*
- **Visual loop:** the last frame should match the first: the same clip, the same zoom level or the same colour. Avoid a freeze, black frame or end card at the very end.
- **Audio loop:** no silence at the end. Music, if any, should cut on a beat that matches the start.
- Creators claim retention above 100% and that "replays are weighted 2–3× on TikTok" *(both unverified)*. The consistent caveat is that a loop only helps a video people already watch to the end.

## 3. Format and production details

- **Length:** 30–60 s is safe for explainers, and the 2026 trend leans to "longer shorts" of 45–90 s *if* retention holds. Sources conflict.
- **On-screen text:** essential, since a large share of viewing is sound-off *(the "85%" figure is unverified)*. TikTok suggests 5–10 words per second when using text.
- **Safe zone (1080×1920):** keep text roughly in the central 1080×1420 area. Leave about 220 px clear at the top and about 450 px at the bottom, where the caption, buttons and audio label sit. Sources disagree, so check with a test upload.
- **Sound:** TikTok lists sound or music as a basic. Trending audio has to be added in the app at upload; it can't be baked in.
- **Formats working in 2026:** faceless explainers, "process with a satisfying payoff", POV and relatable stories, and series with cliffhangers (part 1/2/3).

## 4. What this means for ArticL (today vs. proposed)

| # | Element | ArticL today | Proposed change |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Text hook at 0 s | Hook is spoken; the first on-screen text is the captions | A big hook headline card for the first ~2 s (the script writes a ≤7-word hook title) |
| 2 | Hook type | One generic hook rule in the prompt | The prompt picks from research hook formulas: surprising number, counter-intuitive claim, question, "a study of N people found" |
| 3 | Open loop | None | The hook teases the strongest finding; the script saves it for the last beat |
| 4 | Re-hooks | None | A short transition line before beats 3 and 5 ("but here's the twist") |
| 5 | Pattern interrupts | ~3 s cuts, motion, number callouts: **already good** | Keep; maybe add a sound "whoosh"/hit on callouts |
| 6 | **Loop ending** | 1.2 s **freeze hold** at the end (`END_HOLD_S`) breaks any loop | Closing line written to flow into the hook; the last shot reuses the first clip's look; remove the hold |
| 7 | Engagement prompt | Closing is a summary | The closing ends with a comment question or "send this to…" (drives sends per reach) |
| 8 | Safe zones | Captions at y 820–1180, callouts at y 330–750: **inside the safe area** | Keep; verify the hook card also stays in the safe area |
| 9 | Music | None | A quiet royalty-free bed (Pixabay Music is a candidate) or leave it to in-app audio at posting |
| 10 | Originality | Stock clips + AI voice: the riskiest category | Lean on our own visuals: number callouts, simple data charts from the paper, quote cards. This is also the honest way to show findings |
| 11 | Source credit | Not shown | Credit **without breaking the loop**: small persistent text ("Margalit & Shayo, 2021") instead of an end card |
| 12 | Posting text | None | Generate a post caption + keywords + hashtags alongside the video (TikTok search) |
